Frequently asked questions

Does this remove all metadata, including AI-specific tags?

Yes, for the formats we support. AIMetaScrub removes EXIF, XMP, IPTC, C2PA content credentials and PNG text chunks, including prompts saved by tools like Stable Diffusion and ComfyUI. The image pixels are not touched. The color profile is kept by default so colors look the same.

Can it remove invisible or visual AI watermarks?

No. It cleans metadata only. Some tools, such as Google’s SynthID, embed a signal in the pixels, and that survives metadata cleaning. Visible logos or marks are part of the picture, so they stay too.

Do you store my images? How long are files kept?

No. Nothing is uploaded. Your image is read and cleaned inside your browser tab, held only in memory, and gone when you close or refresh the page. We never see your files.

What formats and limits are supported?

JPG/JPEG, PNG and WebP, up to 5 images at a time. Because everything runs on your device there is no upload limit, but very large files depend on your device’s memory. HEIC and TIFF are not supported yet.

Will removing the metadata remove the AI label on Instagram?

Not necessarily. Platforms read signals in the file, such as C2PA records, the IPTC digital source type and software names in XMP or EXIF, and AIMetaScrub removes those. But metadata is not the only signal. Invisible watermarks like SynthID stay in the pixels, platforms may also rely on creator disclosure, and a post that is already live keeps its label until you replace it. If your image is realistically AI-generated, follow the platform’s rules on labeling it.

Does cleaning change the picture or its quality?

No. We don’t re-encode or resize the image. The picture data is copied byte for byte and only the metadata blocks are cut out, so the file just gets smaller by the amount of metadata it carried.

Does it work on images from ChatGPT, DALL·E, Firefly or Gemini?

It removes the metadata those tools write into JPG, PNG and WebP files, including C2PA records and XMP/IPTC fields. Open the metadata view first to see exactly what your file contains. Gemini and Imagen images also carry SynthID in the pixels, which no metadata tool can remove.

Can I clean HEIC photos from my iPhone?

Not yet. On iPhone you can go to Settings, then Camera, then Formats, and choose Most Compatible. New photos are then saved as JPEG, and you can clean those here.

Why did Instagram put an AI label on my real photo?

Platforms often rely on tags stored in the file rather than the picture itself. Editors such as Lightroom and Photoshop write an AI tag when you use features like Generative Fill, and some phone gallery apps and online editors do too. The tag is usually a C2PA record or the IPTC digital source type. Open the metadata view to see which of these your file carries.

Does the tool scan my caption or prompt text for AI words?

No. It only reads data stored inside the image file and never sees captions you type on social platforms. It looks for known generator names, such as Midjourney or ChatGPT, in the file’s metadata fields, and for prompts that tools like Stable Diffusion or ComfyUI save in PNG files. That can include description or keyword fields stored in the file itself.
